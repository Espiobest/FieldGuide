# Demo Estuary Group: water-level training procedure

Organization: Demo Estuary Group (fictional)
Revision: 2026-02
Purpose: synthetic data for software demonstrations, not an operational field protocol.

## Preparing a visit

Before entering the training area, the field lead checks the tide forecast and the
planned exit route. Work in pairs during this exercise. If the exit route becomes
flooded, stop the exercise and follow the field lead's directions.

## Recording a reading

Record the station identifier, date, local time with UTC offset, and observer initials.
Read the training staff gauge and record the water level in centimeters relative to
the marked training datum. Include a photograph of the gauge with each reading.
This training document does not specify a sampling interval.

## Instrument problems

If the staff gauge is damaged or unreadable, record NA and describe the problem.
Notify the field lead before leaving the station. Do not substitute an estimated
water level for an unreadable gauge.

## Data review

The field lead checks that each reading includes its station identifier, units,
timestamp, UTC offset, and photograph. Preserve the original recorded value when
correcting a transcription error; record the correction and the reason separately.
