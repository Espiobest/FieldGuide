# Demo Marsh Lab: vegetation training procedure

Organization: Demo Marsh Lab (fictional)
Revision: 2026-01
Purpose: synthetic data for software demonstrations, not an operational field protocol.

## Before the survey

The trainee and field lead review the site map together before leaving the staging area.
The field lead confirms the plot identifiers and assigns the trainee a recording role.
Pack a quadrat frame, a field notebook, a pencil, and a camera for the training exercise.

## Vegetation observations

For this fictional exercise, place a 1 m by 1 m quadrat at each marked training plot.
Record the plot identifier, survey date, observer initials, and the species observed.
Estimate the percent cover of each species separately. Overlapping plant layers mean
that the summed species cover may exceed 100 percent.

If a plant cannot be identified, assign a temporary code and photograph it. Ask the
field lead to review the identification before replacing the temporary code. Do not
guess a species name or collect a specimen for this exercise.

## End-of-day review

The trainee and field lead review the notebook together on the same day. Mark an
unmeasured value as NA and add a reason in the notes. Do not enter zero for an
unmeasured value. Zero cover means the species was assessed and was absent.
Store the photographs with the corresponding plot identifier in the filename.
